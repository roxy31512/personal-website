How Sessions Work

1

You Send Me Your Draft and Assignment

Share your essay assignment, grading scale and criteria, and related instructions and your writing draft, even a rough outline or a first paragraph.

2

Structure & Argument Review

We work through the thesis and the logic holding the essay together, making sure each paragraph is doing real work toward the argument.

3

Line Edits & Source Use

Detailed feedback on clarity, tone, and how evidence is integrated, plus notes on citation and academic style.

4

Final Pass

A last read-through before submission to catch anything that still needs tightening.

Good to Know

Session Format
Live sessions are one-on-one over video call, online only. Async feedback is sent and returned by email, no call needed. I'm not teaching course material: the focus is your essay, your argument, and your writing.
Turnaround Time
For async written feedback, expect to hear back within 24-48 hours of sending your draft.
Cancellations & Rescheduling
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a live session. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed without notice, are charged in full.
Payment
Payment is due before each session or package, by Interac e-Transfer. I'll send transfer details once we've confirmed a booking.
