How Sessions Work

1

Send Me Your Draft or Prompt

Share your essay prompt and whatever you have so far, even a rough outline or a first paragraph. No draft is too early.

2

Structure & Argument Review

We work through the thesis and the logic holding the essay together, making sure each paragraph is doing real work toward the argument.

3

Line Edits & Source Use

Detailed feedback on clarity, tone, and how evidence is integrated, plus notes on citation and academic style.

4

Final Pass

A last read-through before submission to catch anything that still needs tightening.

Good to Know

Session Format
Sessions are one-on-one, mostly held online over video call. In-person sessions are available in London or Winnipeg by arrangement. I'm not teaching course material: the focus is your essay, your argument, and your writing.
Turnaround Time
If you send a draft for written feedback rather than a live session, expect to hear back within 24-48 hours.
Cancellations & Rescheduling
Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed without notice, are charged in full.
Payment
Payment is due before each session or package, by Interac e-Transfer. I'll send transfer details once we've confirmed a booking.
